# FNVR motion controls guide

This is the default right-handed layout. With **Left-handed** turned on (setup screen or Body & profiles), the gun is held and fired with your **left** hand and the right hand supports it. The sticks and face buttons stay where they are: left stick moves, right stick turns, A/B on the right, X/Y on the left.

"Grip" is the side button under your middle finger. A "fist" is grip + trigger squeezed together.

## Valve Index, Vive and Windows Mixed Reality controllers

These are read through SteamVR's controller bindings, with FNVR's own default binding for each:

- **Valve Index:** the thumbstick moves and turns (the trackpad does nothing unless you bind it), **squeezing** the handle is the grip (just resting your fingers on it isn't), A and B are A/X and B/Y, and every finger is tracked.
- **Vive wands:** the trackpad is the stick, clicking it is A/X, the menu button is B/Y.
- **Windows Mixed Reality:** the thumbstick moves and turns, clicking the trackpad is A/X, the menu button is B/Y.

To change any button: SteamVR > Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller Bindings, then pick FNVR. Quest, Rift, Reverb G2 and Cosmos controllers are read directly, as before. Controls page > Controller input switches this (used from the next start).

## Seated play

With **Play style: Seated** (setup screen or Body & profiles):

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sit up straight | Your character stands at full height |
| Hold right stick **down** | Crouch (reach low things); let go to come back up |
| Everything else | The same as standing |

## Walking around

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left stick | Move |
| Left stick click (hold) | Sprint |
| Swing both arms like running | Move where you look (swing hard to sprint) |
| Right stick left/right | Turn |
| Right A | Jump |
| Right stick click | Sneak (crouch) |
| Really crouch or lean | Your view and arms follow (reach the floor, lean over things) |

## Fighting

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Point your right hand | Aim. Shots go where the hand points, not where you look |
| Right trigger | Fire / attack (revolvers fire as fast as you pull) |
| Swing your hand fast | Melee or punch (the trigger can attack too - setting; automatic melee weapons use the trigger) |
| Right B | Reload |
| Left hand on the gun (or melee handle) + left grip | Two-handed grip (steadies the gun / swings the weapon with both hands) |
| Raise a scoped gun to your eye | Scope view |
| Left trigger | Bullet time (with VATS Alternative - Bullet Time), or aim down sights if that option is on |

## Hands and objects

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right grip | Activate: talk, open doors and containers, pick up items |
| Either grip next to a loose object (no weapon out) | Grab it and hold it in that hand |
| Let go of the grip while moving your hand | Throw the held object |
| Grip on a ledge or wall | Climb |
| Just move your hands | Your hands knock loose objects around |

## Body gestures

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right grip at your right hip | Draw or holster a pistol / one-handed melee weapon |
| Right grip over your right shoulder | Draw or holster a long gun / two-handed melee weapon |
| Free hand's grip at the middle of your chest | Pull out a **Stimpak** (or Super Stimpak) if you have one - otherwise the pause menu |
| Stab the stimpak into your other forearm or your chest | Use it: heals like the game's own stimpak (skills and perks count) |
| Let go of a stimpak without stabbing | It goes back into your inventory |
| Left X | Pause menu too |
| Raise your left wrist to your face | Open the Pip-Boy. Lower it to close |
| Tap left Y | Open the Pip-Boy with its menu floating in front of your face. Tap again to close |
| Hold left Y | Pip-Boy light on/off |

You feel a short buzz when your hand enters a holster or chest spot.

## Grappling hook

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left grip, then left trigger | Fire the grapple where your left hand points; you get pulled there (trigger first = bullet time instead) |
| Right grip, then right trigger | Fire with the right hand. Only when no gun is out, since with a gun the trigger shoots. Turn on in settings |
| Hook a person or creature | Pulls them to you; they're knocked down when they arrive (let go to drop them) |
| Let go before you arrive | Launch yourself with the speed you've built up |
| Fire the other hand mid-pull | Swing on to a new point |

All of this is set under Settings → Movement & comfort:
- Range, top speed, start speed, time to top speed, launch strength, end hop and air slowdown.
- Rope on or off, rumble, hooking onto people, and safe landing.

## Pip-Boy (wrist open)

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right A | Select |
| Right B | Back |
| Left X | The Pip-Boy's X action (e.g. drop) |
| Left Y | Close the Pip-Boy |
| Left grip / right grip | The Pip-Boy's left / right bumper buttons |
| Left stick | Scroll / move the cursor |
| Right stick | The game's right stick: moves the map cursor (pick a fast-travel spot), scrolls |
| Triggers | The Pip-Boy's trigger buttons |
| Stick clicks | The Pip-Boy's stick-click buttons |
| **F10** (keyboard) | Switch between the world pausing and the world keeping running while the Pip-Boy is open |

### Moving the menu on the Pip-Boy screen
With the Pip-Boy open, **click the left stick** to start adjusting:

| Control | Slide mode (starts here) | Tilt mode |
|---|---|---|
| Left stick | Slide the menu along your arm (left/right, up/down) | Tilt and turn it |
| Right stick up/down | Bigger / smaller | Taller / shorter |
| Right stick left/right | Closer / further from the screen | Turn |
| Right A | Switch to tilt mode | Switch to slide mode |
| Left X | Reset to the default spot | Quarter turn |
| Right B | **Save** and stop adjusting | **Save** and stop adjusting |

Once saved, the menu stays in that spot every time.

| Right A or right grip | Select |

## Pop-up menus (talking, trading, containers)

The world keeps running while these are open, and your character holds still.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right A | Select |
| Left X | Accept the trade (vendor menu) / take all (containers) |
| Turn your head well away (75° by default) | Close the menu |

Message boxes with an OK button (tutorial prompts, quest messages) are normal menus: they pause the game and A or the trigger presses OK.

Size, height and the look-away angle are on the **HUD & menus** settings page.

## HUD layout

Pick it on the startup screen (**HUD**) or under **HUD & menus → HUD layout**:

- **Classic:** the game's HUD in front of you, as before.
- **VR:** the compass sits on the back of your Pip-Boy hand (just in front of the Pip-Boy), health and AP sit on your other wrist like a watch, and each gun's ammo shows right beside the hand holding it (turned to face you, to the inside so it doesn't cover the sights). Turn your wrist toward you to read it.
- **Custom:** your own choices, set under **HUD & menus → HUD parts: move & resize**:

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| Part | Which part the rows below change: compass, health, action points, ammo (gun hand), other hand's weapon, sneak indicator. It pulses while you change it. |
| Show it | Off: the part isn't shown anywhere. |
| Where | On the HUD (where the game draws it), In front of you (its own small panel that moves with your head), Left wrist, Right wrist, Left hand, Right hand |
| Size | Bigger or smaller (100% = its normal size for that spot) |
| Move left / right, down / up, in / out | Nudge it into place (centimetres). On a wrist, left/right is along your forearm and in/out is off your skin; by a hand, in/out is forward and back. |
| Reset this part's size and spot | Back to 100% and no nudges |

Changing any part makes the layout **Custom**. Picking Classic or VR again sets every part back to that layout. The whole HUD can still be switched off with **Show the HUD**. (Parts off the HUD need **Curved menus and HUD**, which is on as standard.)

## Quick-loot window (looking at a container or body)

Needs Just Assorted Mods' quick-loot menu.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right A | Take the selected item (sends the mod's **E** key) |
| Right B | Take everything (sends the mod's take-all key) |
| Right stick up/down | Scroll the list (it never turns you while the window is up) |
| Right grip or left Y | Open the full container menu |

The take keys are sent as keyboard presses, so the game window must be the active window on your PC.

## Setup and calibration

When the game starts, the setup screen asks who's playing, standing or seated, and calibrates a new profile. Point and pull the trigger, or use the sticks; **Start playing** goes to the main menu.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hold **both thumbsticks in** ~1 second (or **F11**) | Open or close the VR settings panel |
| **Click both thumbsticks** while pointing the right controller at the centre of your view | Calibrate the aim angle (also re-centres your standing height and lean) |
| Settings → Body & profiles → Calibrate my body | Stand straight, then hold both arms out: sets height and arm reach |

## Keyboard keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| F3 / F4 | Move where shots land left / right by half a degree (saved) |
| F6 / F7 | Move where shots land down / up by half a degree (saved) |
| F8 | Head tracking on/off |
| F9 | Fill the whole headset view on/off |
| F10 | Pip-Boy: world keeps running / world pauses |
| Ctrl+F10 | 3D view: full 3D → alternating eyes → 2D (flat) |
| F11 | VR settings panel |
